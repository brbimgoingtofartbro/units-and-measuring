Units and Measuring

Alayne Morris · Centimetres (cm) and millimetres (mm)

Worksheet 1: Read the ruler

Time: 3:00

Look at each ruler and read where the arrow points. Enter your answer in millimetres (mm). Each small line is 1 mm, and 10 mm = 1 cm.

Worksheet 2: Convert cm and mm

Time: 3:00

Remember: cm to mm: multiply by 10. mm to cm: divide by 10. Type the number only; the unit is shown in the question.
